A city from hill to river

Begin above the red roofs of Alfama, descend through historic Baixa for a traditional lunch, follow the Tagus waterfront home, and finish with inventive Portuguese petiscos in Chiado.

8:45 AM

Depart the hotel

Leave 54 São Paulo on Rua da Boavista. Take an 8–10 minute Uber or Bolt uphill to Alfama. Tram 28 is the scenic alternative.

Ride · 8–10 min
9:00 AM

Alfama wakes up

Wander the quiet cobblestone alleys of Lisbon's oldest neighborhood. Stop at a local pastelaria for coffee and a fresh pastry.

Neighborhood stroll · 1 hour
10:00 AM

Miradouro de Santa Luzia

Pause for sweeping views across Alfama's red roofs to the Tagus River, framed by gardens and blue-and-white azulejos.

Panoramic viewpoint · 30 min
10:30 AM

Castelo de São Jorge

Walk five minutes uphill to the fortress, then explore the medieval ramparts, gardens, and 360-degree city views.

Castle visit · about 1 hour
11:30 AM

Descend toward Rossio

Take the scenic 15-minute downhill route from the castle through upper Baixa. Reach A Provinciana around 11:45 AM to line up before its noon opening.

Timing tip: Arriving 15 minutes early gives you the best chance of getting a table as the doors open.

Downhill walk · 15 min
12:00–1:30

Traditional lunch at A Provinciana

Settle in for home-style Portuguese classics in an unpretentious neighborhood dining room.

Lunch · 1½ hours
1:30 PM

Baixa to the waterfront

Stroll down pedestrian Rua Augusta, pass beneath the triumphal arch, and emerge into grand Praça do Comércio.

Architecture + city stroll
3:00 PM

Ribeira das Naus

Follow the flat riverside promenade toward Cais do Sodré. Pause at a kiosk for a cold imperial or Vinho Verde, or browse nearby shops.

Riverside walk + drinks
5:30 PM

Return and reset

Walk two blocks inland to the hotel. Relax, shower, and dress for dinner from approximately 6:00–7:45 PM.

Hotel break · about 2 hours
8:00 PM

Dinner at Ofício

At 7:45 PM, walk about 10 minutes uphill into Chiado, or take a four-minute rideshare. Dinner is modern Portuguese petiscos at this Michelin Bib Gourmand restaurant.
