Why leave early?

Leaving Lisbon around 7:30 AM is the ideal Sintra strategy. You will beat the heaviest crowds, reach Pena Park as it opens at 9:00 AM, and still return to Lisbon in the early afternoon.

7:30 AM

Catch the train from Lisbon

Board at Rossio Station for the roughly 40-minute direct ride to Sintra.

Check the timetable: Typical departures include 7:11 or 7:41 AM on weekdays, and 7:01 or 8:01 AM on weekends and holidays.

Train · about 40 min
8:15 AM

Arrive and ride up to Pena

Take a 10–15 minute rideshare such as Uber or Bolt, or catch the local bus from Sintra station directly to Pena Palace. Arriving around 8:40 AM puts you at the gates just before opening.

Transfer · 10–15 min
9:00–10:30

Pena Park and outer terraces

Explore the parklands, viewpoint balconies, and colorful exterior palace walls before the tour buses arrive.

Palace views · 1½ hours
10:45 AM

Walk down to Quinta da Regaleira

Follow the scenic downhill walking path from the palace gates toward Quinta da Regaleira.

Scenic walk · about 20 min
11:00–12:30

Quinta da Regaleira gardens

Head straight for the Initiation Well, the Poço Initiático, before the main midday queues form. Then wander through the surrounding grottoes and garden trails.

Gardens · 1½ hours
12:45–2:00

Sit-down lunch in town

Walk about five minutes into Sintra's historic center. Try Tascantiga for traditional Portuguese petiscos, or Incomum near the station.

Lunch · 1¼ hours
2:15 PM

Return to Lisbon

Pick up a quick travesseiro pastry at Casa Piriquita, then walk to Sintra station for the train back to Rossio.

Pastry stop + train

Lisbon by late afternoon

You should arrive at Rossio Station around 3:00 PM, leaving the rest of your afternoon and evening wide open.

Boa viagem! Have a wonderful trip to Sintra.
