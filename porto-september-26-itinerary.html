City to cellar

Drop your bags, trace Porto's architectural landmarks to a local tavern lunch, then follow the natural downhill route to the Douro. Cross into Gaia for port wine and climb to a golden-hour viewpoint before dinner.

11:00 AM

Arrive at Porto São Bento

Continue from São Bento Station to Hotel Vincci Bonjardim by a quick taxi or metro ride north.

Arrival + transfer
11:20 AM

Bag drop and refresh

Leave your luggage with the front desk at Hotel Vincci Bonjardim and get ready to explore on foot.

Hotel stop · free
11:45 AM

Clérigos and Igreja do Carmo

Walk through the Clérigos district and admire Igreja do Carmo's celebrated blue-and-white tiled wall. Climb Clérigos Tower if time and energy allow.

Optional climb: The church is free; the tower is approximately €8.

Architectural walk · 1 hour
12:45 PM

Lunch at Casa Expresso

Settle into this compact, no-frills local tasca at Praça de Carlos Alberto 73. Look for hearty soup, fresh grilled fish, or traditional rojões, marinated diced pork.

Local rhythm: Seating is first come, first served. The room is lively and popular with workers; pair lunch with a young house Vinho Verde.

Lunch · about €8–€12 per plate
2:15 PM

Downhill to the Ribeira

Stroll through Porto's historic alleys toward the river. Rua das Flores offers a lively, pedestrian-friendly route down to Cais da Ribeira.

Scenic downhill walk
3:30 PM

Cross the Dom Luís I Bridge

Walk across the upper deck into Vila Nova de Gaia for magnificent panoramic views over the Douro, Ribeira, and port-wine district.

Bridge walk · free
4:00 PM

Port cellar tasting

Choose one of Gaia's historic port lodges along the waterfront for a guided tasting and an introduction to the region's signature fortified wine.

Plan ahead: Tastings generally begin around €15–€20. Check tour times and reserve a cellar if you have a favorite.

Wine tasting · about 2 hours
6:30 PM

Golden hour above the Douro

Head to Jardim do Morro or Serra do Pilar for sunset, broad city views, and the street music that often fills the hilltop.

Sunset viewpoint · free
8:00 PM

Dinner at Fauno

Finish the arrival day with your confirmed reservation and a five-course tasting menu at Fauno Restaurant.

Reserved · 5-course menu

A route without backtracking

The day follows Porto's natural geography: from the hotel through Clérigos to lunch, downhill along Rua das Flores, across the river into Gaia, and finally upward for sunset.

Bom passeio! Enjoy your first day in Porto.
